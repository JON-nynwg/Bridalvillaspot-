About BridalVilla Spot

BridalVilla Spot is a makeup, hair and beauty studio on the sixth floor of Westlands Business Park, Nairobi. Brides, bridal parties and anyone who wants to feel their best can get it all done in one welcoming place.

Our team works across makeup, braids and locs, wigs and nails, so your whole look comes together in one visit. Tell us the look you have in mind and we will help you plan it.

  • Bridal makeup & party preparation
  • Braids, locs & wig styling
  • Makeup, nails & finishing touches
